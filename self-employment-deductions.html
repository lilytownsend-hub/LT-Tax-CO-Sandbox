Self-Employment Deduction Guide

Use this as a year-round reminder of expenses that may matter at tax time. Check items that apply to your business and keep the receipts, invoices, mileage logs, statements, and other records that support them.

Important: An item being listed here does not automatically make it deductible. In general, a business expense must be ordinary and necessary for the business, and personal portions must be separated when an expense has mixed business/personal use.
