I Had Marketplace Insurance. Where's My Tax Form?

If you or someone in your household had Marketplace health insurance, the tax form you're usually looking for is Form 1095-A. You may be able to download it now instead of waiting for another copy in the mail.

HealthCare.gov

  1. Sign in to your Marketplace account.
  2. Select the application for the tax year you're filing—not the current coverage year.
  3. Select Tax Forms.
  4. Under Your Form 1095-A, choose Download PDF.
  5. If you see a form marked Corrected, use the corrected version instead of the earlier one.

HealthCare.gov 1095-A Help

Minnesota / MNsure

Sign in to your MNsure account and check Notifications or your Enrollment Dashboard / My Inbox for the 1095-A attachment.

MNsure 1095-A Instructions

Don't race to file without it. If you had Marketplace coverage, make sure you have an accurate 1095-A before filing. You can receive more than one 1095-A if plans, household members, or coverage changed during the year. Form 1095-A information is used with Form 8962 to reconcile the Premium Tax Credit when required.

If your coverage came through a state Marketplace other than HealthCare.gov or MNsure, use that Marketplace's official account or support site to retrieve the form.
