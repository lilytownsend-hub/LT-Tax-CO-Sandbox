Notices & Letters

I Got a Letter From the IRS. Now What?

An IRS letter can look intimidating before you even know what it says. Start with the reason for the notice—not the size of the envelope.

Before responding: keep the entire notice, note the response deadline, and don't send original records unless the instructions specifically require them. If you're unsure what the notice is asking, get help before guessing.
