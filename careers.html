Client Service Associate (CSA)

Seasonal · January–April · Janesville, Wisconsin · $17/hour

The Client Service Associate helps manage the day-to-day client experience and administrative flow of the tax office. This role is a good fit for someone who is organized, comfortable working independently, and takes client confidentiality seriously.

What You'll Do

  • Welcome clients and assist with client intake.
  • Answer phones and help manage scheduling.
  • Receive, scan, organize, and securely handle client tax documents.
  • Help maintain client status, notes, and the office workflow queue.
  • Assist with routine payment and invoice status questions.
  • Protect confidential client and financial information.
  • Route tax questions to the tax preparer rather than providing tax advice.
  • Help keep the office organized and running smoothly.

What We're Looking For

  • Dependable and comfortable working independently.
  • Organized and able to manage multiple priorities.
  • Professional and comfortable communicating with clients.
  • Good judgment when handling mistakes, questions, or unfamiliar situations.
  • Strong respect for privacy and confidentiality.
  • Comfortable using computers and learning office software.
