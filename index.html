How It Works

1. Get StartedNew clients complete the tax prep estimator and request portal access. Once your secure client portal is activated, complete your tax questionnaire.
2. Gather & UploadUse your personalized checklist to see what's needed and securely upload your tax documents through your portal.
3. We Prepare Your ReturnWe'll review your information and prepare your return. If we have questions or need anything additional, we'll reach out.
4. Review & SignReview your completed return, ask any questions you have, and securely sign the required documents.
5. File & You're DoneOnce everything is approved, signed, and paid, your return will be electronically filed. Your final tax documents will remain available through your secure client portal, and you'll receive confirmation once your return has been accepted.
