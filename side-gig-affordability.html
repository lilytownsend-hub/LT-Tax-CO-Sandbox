Can I Afford This Side Gig?

Revenue is not profit, and profit is not the same thing as money you actually get to keep. This quick calculator puts those numbers next to each other.

This is a planning tool, not a tax-return calculation. Your actual federal and state tax depends on your total household income, filing status, deductions, credits, and other facts. Use the reserve percentage as a planning assumption—not a promised tax rate.
