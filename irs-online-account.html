Your Federal Tax Records

What Does the IRS Know About Me?

Your IRS Individual Online Account can answer a surprising number of “where do I find that?” tax questions without waiting on hold or digging through old paperwork.

Transcripts

Access available tax return, account, and wage-and-income transcripts.

Payments & balances

Review certain payment history, balances, and payment-related information tied to your federal tax account.

Notices & records

Some notices and tax records can be available through the account, depending on the year and item.

IP PIN

If you're enrolled in the Identity Protection PIN program, your current IP PIN can be retrieved through your account.

Go to IRS Individual Online Account

Why set it up before you need it? Because the worst time to learn where your tax records live is when you're already missing a document, responding to a notice, or trying to file.
